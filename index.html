      Privacy

      Short version: no ads, no tracking, no selling of data. An account is optional — everything except rankings and online badges works without one.

      On your own device

      Your solves, times, algorithm progress, settings and colours are saved in this browser only. They never leave your device unless you export a backup. You can delete them by clearing this site's data in your browser.

      If you create an account

      Stored in our database (Supabase): your e-mail address and password (stored securely hashed — nobody can read it), your username, avatar, MMR, wins/losses, best single, badges and the results of your ranked 1v1 rounds.

      Your e-mail is only used to let you sign in. It is never shown to other players, never shared, and never used for newsletters or advertising. Your username, avatar, MMR, wins/losses and badges are public (leaderboard).

      Feature requests

      When you send an idea, we store your message, the optional name/e-mail you add, and (if you're signed in) your account ID — only to read and answer it.

      1v1 games

      Players connect directly to each other (WebRTC). The free PeerJS service is used to find each other with the game code; it sees your IP address while connecting. Cube moves and times go directly between the devices in the game (and to people watching) and are not stored — except the result of ranked rounds, as described above.

      Services this site uses

      • GitHub Pages — hosts the website
      • Supabase — accounts, rankings and feature requests
      • Cloudflare Turnstile — the robot check at sign-in (it looks at technical signals from your browser to stop bots)
      • PeerJS — connecting 1v1 players
      • jsDelivr and Google Fonts — deliver code libraries and fonts

      Like any website, these services can see your IP address when your browser contacts them.

      Your rights

      You can see your account data in your profile and change your username and avatar there. You can delete your account yourself at any time: profile → Delete my account. That removes your e-mail, profile, badges and match reports immediately; past ranked results stay in your opponents' history without your name. For any other question or request (for example a copy of your data), use “Request a feature” and leave a way to reach you.

      You also have the right to complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority (gegevensbeschermingsautoriteit.be).
